# Product brief — expanded spatial design studio

**Status:** pipeline intake; subject to evidence and critic review  
**Source run:** `20260718-spatial-design-studio`  
**Opened:** 2026-07-18

## Promise

Show Interiorin an empty or existing interior or exterior space. It builds a visibly calibrated spatial starting point, proposes coherent directions, explains which choices suit the space, and lets the person refine known elements conversationally. Named versions turn exploration into a decision that a homeowner, partner, architect, designer, or contractor can understand together.

## Core journey

1. Choose **Interior** or **Exterior** and provide a photograph, floor plan, or guided set of views.
2. Confirm one known measurement or explicitly continue with an approximate model.
3. Inspect what Interiorin recognized, protected, inferred, and could not know.
4. Generate two spatially grounded design directions.
5. Explore the canonical scene in interactive 3D; use optional generated concept imagery only as a presentation view.
6. Speak or type refinements such as moving a known object, changing a material zone, adjusting light, or replacing a known planting/furnishing category.
7. Save named options and compare real captures plus factual scene diffs.
8. Export an assumption/change summary for professional review.

## Provider posture

- **ElevenLabs** is the preferred voice-experience candidate because its current browser WebRTC and client-tool model can support low-latency conversational control. It remains behind a provider adapter.
- **OpenAI** is a candidate for structured spatial understanding and/or realtime agent reasoning.
- **Gemini image generation (Nano Banana family)** is a candidate for optional concept imagery and controlled visual editing; it must never become the hidden geometry source of truth.
- **21st.dev** is configured as development-time component inspiration/tooling, while the final interface must follow the project’s approved design system and accessibility contract.

## Truth boundary

Interiorin is an early decision and communication tool, not a replacement for surveys, CAD/BIM documentation, engineering, drainage analysis, local-code review, or licensed professional judgment.
